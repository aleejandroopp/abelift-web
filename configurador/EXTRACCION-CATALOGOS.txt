The strength and drive of Tresa | La force et la motivation de Tresa
Marichu Echevarria es la mejor 
comercial que Tresa ha tenido a 
lo largo de su historia. Y es que, a 
principios de los años 90, mien-
tras su marido Gonzalo Suárez 
buscaba la fórmula perfecta para 
crear los mejores ascensores, ella 
recorría los edificios de Gijón de 
cuatro plantas que sabía que no 
tenían ascensor. “Lo hacía a la 
hora de la sobremesa, cuando las 
mujeres de las casas ya habían 
acabado de comer y recoger y to-
maban el café antes de salir a sus 
quehaceres”. Allí, compartiendo 
la sobremesa con ellas, les ofrecía  
poner ascensor en su edificio. Les 
convencía de que priorizaran esta 
obra frente a otras inversiones en 
el hogar, y así, con buen criterio 
y grandes dotes de venta, cerraba 
los negocios. 
Si Gonzalo, su marido, es la tem-
planza y el conocimiento, Marichu 
es la fuerza y el empuje. “Esto 
existe porque existe Marichu”, 
resume Gonzalo. 
Ella, como años más tarde haría 
con las amas de casa gijonesas, 
fue la que convenció a su marido 
de poner en marcha la empresa. 
“Es que yo sabía mucho mejor 
que él de lo que él era capaz”, 
explica. Ella es la que todavía hoy 
en día, recorre fábrica y oficina 
con una mirada crítica y auditora, 
trasmitiendo ímpetu y ganas a los 
empleados que la miran con el 
respeto ganado con los años.
Marichu Echevarria is the best 
sales representative Tresa has 
had throughout its history. And, 
after all, in the early 90s, while 
her husband Gonzalo Suárez was 
looking for the perfect formula to 
make the best lifts, she wandered 
around four-storey buildings in 
Gijón that she knew didn’t have 
a lift. “I did so just after lunchti-
me, when housewives were done 
having lunch and tidying up while 
drinking a coffee before going out 
to do their things”. There, having 
a chat with them, I offered them 
to install a lift in their buildings. 
I convinced them to prioritise 
these works over other household 
investments. Like this, with good 
criteria and great selling skills, 
she closed deals. 
If Gonzalo her husband, repre-
sents temperance and knowledge, 
Marichu represents strength and 
drive. “We are here thanks to Ma-
richu”, summarises Gonzalo. 
It was she, as she did years later 
with housewives in Gijon, who 
convinced her husband to set the 
company into motion. “And it was 
because no one knew better than 
me what he was capable of”, she 
explains. Nowadays it is she who 
still wanders around the factory 
and the office with a critical and 
auditing eye, transferring her im-
petus and will to employees who 
look up to her due to the respect 
she has gained over the years.

Gonzalo Suárez pasea la mirada 
por los modernos y acristalados 
despachos de la fábrica recién

En sus ojos, orgullo. Mucho 
ha cambiado la empresa que

Tresa, es, en definitiva, mucho

Gonzalo Suárez lets his eyes 
wander over the modern glass 
offices in the recently opened 
factory  of Ascensores Tresa. 
There is pride in his eyes. A lot 
has changed in the company 
that he, with his wife Marichu, 
established in 1983 in a small 
30-sqm office. It has today 
become large  headquarters 
of more than 10,000 sqm. 
His eyes also hide the safety 
of knowing the secret of this 
success: “Ambition. But we-
ll-understood ambition. The last 
thing is to earn money. The first 
and foremost thing is to become 
the best”. 
With this goal of being the best, 
Ascensores Tresa soon repla-
ced this office from which they 
maintained lifts with a small

started manufacturing their own 
lifts under one premise: quality 
above all, but hand in hand with

have treasury stock... 
“The world is quite changed.

Gonzalo Suárez explains. For 
him, Tresa is, after all, much

épouse Marichu en 1983 et qui 
n’était qu’un petit bureau de

une seule idée: la qualité par 
dessus tout, mais en la conju-
guant aux bons prix, une bonne 
commercialisation, la capacité 
d’avoir des fonds propres... 
« Le monde a beaucoup chan-

ascenseurs, mais nous avons su 
nous adapter, atteindre davan-
tage de pays, créer de nouveaux 
produits », explique Gonzalo 
Suárez, pour qui Tresa représente 
beaucoup plus qu’une simple 
entreprise.
